The Carhop Way

Your Window. Your Tray. Your Seat.

Pull in, roll down the window, and let us take it from there. A carhop brings your order out on a tray that clips right to your car door — the way diners have done it since the days of fin-tailed cruisers on Main Street.

Prefer to stretch your legs? Step inside to the retro dining room, grab a chrome stool at the counter, or take a seat at the outdoor tables on a sunny Fillmore afternoon.

And keep an eye on the calendar — Filly’s loves a theme. Past seasons have brought full “Christmas in July” takeovers with decorations, music and even a little indoor “snow.”
