Before You Cruise In

Tips For Your Visit

1

Traveling On I-15?

Fillmore sits right along the interstate — take the Main Street side of town and you’ll spot us at 270 N Main St. It’s the classic road-trip pit stop.

2

Go Full Retro

Skip the drive-thru mindset: park in the lot, stay in your seat, and let a carhop bring your order out on a proper window tray.

3

Sunny Day? Sit Outside

Guests love the outdoor seating on warm Utah afternoons — same food, fresh-air view of Main Street.

4

Bringing A Crowd

Big family, team party or bus tour? Give us a ring at (435) 743-5510 and we’ll help you plan the stop.

See You At The Window
